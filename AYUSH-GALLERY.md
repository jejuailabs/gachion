# Ayush — sculptural gallery

Focused redesign of Ayush only. Original titanium artwork generated using built-in imagegen, then animated through the prompt project's existing MiniMax H3 image-to-video workflow. TRELLIS/Blender pipeline documentation was reviewed; no mesh or Blender render was generated for this edition. The delivered visual is a generated cinematic video, not an interactive 3D model.

New art direction: monumental brushed titanium, volcanic basalt, midnight blue architecture, amber raking light. Typography and sculpture occupy separate visual areas. The same dark palette continues through project statistics, the five-step method, photos, and participation links. Real activity photographs and all factual content remain.

## Image prompt — built-in imagegen
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: premium cinematic hero artwork for a Korean community AI landing page; first frame for slow cinematic motion.
Primary request: contemporary digital art exhibition, mature sophisticated material artistry. A monumental sculptural folded titanium ribbon resembling an open unfolding sheet or wing, suspended over a black volcanic basalt slab in a vast midnight blue architectural gallery.
Style/medium: photorealistic high-end art installation photography / exquisite CGI with physically believable materials.
Composition/framing: 3:2 landscape. Sculpture occupies RIGHT 60% of composition, with LEFT 40% deep dark clean negative space reserved for large white Korean type added later. Restrained composition, entire sculptural gesture visible with breathing room. Monumental scale.
Lighting/mood: dramatic raking warm illumination, restrained molten amber edge light, delicate atmospheric haze.
Color palette: brushed silver, iridescent deep cobalt, midnight navy, black basalt, subtle amber.
Materials/textures: fine titanium brushing, delicate real material microtexture, natural rough volcanic stone. Sculptural folds feel elegant and architecturally substantial.
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no people, no random spheres or gadgets. NOT toy-like, NOT cartoon, NOT ordinary computer props.

## H3 motion prompt

Locked camera, no zoom or camera movement. A monumental brushed titanium wing sculpture suspended above a black volcanic basalt plinth in a midnight blue architectural gallery. The silver folded metal sculpture slowly turns and gracefully unfurls through a small deliberate arc, maintaining its sculptural integrity and weight. Warm amber highlights glide across its brushed metallic surface as the sculpture turns, revealing intricate fine texture and cool blue reflections. Thin atmospheric haze drifts gently behind it. Reflected light glimmers softly on the dark polished floor. Sophisticated museum installation cinematography, mature fine art, tactile physically believable metal. Keep the left 40 percent dark and clear. Preserve the architecture and basalt plinth completely stationary. No new objects, no particles, no text, no people, no logos, no cartoon style, no melting. Beautiful measured continuous movement.
